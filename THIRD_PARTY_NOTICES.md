## Apple Calendar bridge

`native/CalendarBridge.swift` is independently implemented using Apple's system EventKit, Foundation and CryptoKit frameworks. No third-party calendar source or Apple Calendar database is bundled. EventKit access follows the system authorization API:

https://developer.apple.com/documentation/eventkit/ekeventstore/requestfullaccesstoevents%28completion%3A%29
